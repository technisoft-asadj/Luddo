**Full description (max 4000):**

Ludo Fight is the classic Ludo board game with a bright, modern look. Roll the dice, race your four pawns around the board, capture your rivals and be the first to bring them all home!

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Vs Computer: three difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard) with 1 to 3 computer opponents.
- Pass & Play: 2, 3 or 4 friends on one phone.
- Online: create a room, share the 6-letter code and play with friends anywhere - or use Quick Match to find players worldwide and climb the ranked and weekly leaderboards.
- Game modes: Classic, Master, Arrow, Blitz and Team Up.
- Busy for a moment? Switch on Auto play during an online game.

TALK WHILE YOU PLAY
- Optional voice chat in online rooms. Mute yourself, mute any player, block or report anyone.

FRIENDS
- Add friends, see who is online and invite them to your room with one tap. Met someone in a match? Tap their picture to send a friend request.

CLASSIC RULES
- Roll a 6 to leave the base, a 6 gives an extra turn, capture opponents to send them home, safe stars protect your pawns, exact roll to finish, three sixes in a row lose the turn.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
- Colourful board, smooth pawn animations, satisfying sounds and vibration (you can turn them off), custom player names and pictures.
- Collect 8 shiny dice designs (they only change the look, never your luck), open a free chest of coins every 4 hours and claim daily rewards.

Free to play with ads (optional rewarded ads give bonus coins). Online features are optional and need an internet connection. For ages 13+.
