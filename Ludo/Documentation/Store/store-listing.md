**Full description (max 4000):**

Ludo Fight is the classic Ludo board game with a bright, modern look. Roll the dice, race your four pawns around the board, capture your rivals and be the first to bring them all home!

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Vs Computer: three difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard) with 1 to 3 computer opponents.
- Pass & Play: 2, 3 or 4 friends on one phone.
- Online: create a room, share the 6-letter code and play with friends anywhere - or use Quick Match to find players worldwide.

TALK WHILE YOU PLAY
- Optional voice chat in online rooms. Mute yourself, mute any player, block or report anyone.

FRIENDS
- Add friends, see who is online and invite them to your room with one tap.

CLASSIC RULES
- Roll a 6 to leave the base, a 6 gives an extra turn, capture opponents to send them home, safe stars protect your pawns, exact roll to finish, three sixes in a row lose the turn.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
- Colourful board, smooth pawn animations, satisfying sounds and vibration (you can turn them off), custom player names and pictures.

Free to play with ads. Online features are optional and need an internet connection. For ages 13+.
